Bruchovsky real data in this folder
==============================

- bruchovsky_psa.csv : synthetic surrogate (generated by src/make_data.py).
- bruchovsky_real.csv : REAL trial extract, converted to
  patient_id,date,psa_ng_ml,on_treatment.

Source of bruchovsky_real.csv:
- Original trial: Bruchovsky et al. Canadian prospective Phase II trial of
  intermittent androgen suppression (2000/2006/2007).
- Download (via Internet Archive, Nov 2020 capture of the author's page):
  http://www.nicholasbruchovsky.com/dataTanaka.zip
  (archived as https://web.archive.org/web/20201113061158/http://www.nicholasbruchovsky.com/dataTanaka.zip)
  Folder Bruchovsky_et_al/, 72 patients, 3461 PSA readings.
- Metadata-only Figshare record (no files):
  https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Data_from_the_Phase_II_trial_of_intermittent_androgen_suppression_for_men_in_biochemical_recurrence_after_radiotherapy_for_locally_advanced_prostate_cancer_by_Bruchovsky_et_al_2006_/16847362/1
- Conversion: dataTanaka patient files -> CSV columns
  (patient file number -> B###, date -> ISO, col 5 PSA, col 8 on/off 1/0;
  rows with missing PSA skipped). See /tmp/convert_bruchovsky.py.
- Patient classifications (relapse etc.): dataTanaka/classificationsofpatients.txt.

Use it with:
  ./.venv/bin/python src/run_pipeline.py --data data/bruchovsky_real.csv
